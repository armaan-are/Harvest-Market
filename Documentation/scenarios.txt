Mark:  

Mark logs into the farm marketplace early in the morning after checking his crop reports. He compares bulk fertilizer prices from multiple verified suppliers, filters by fastest delivery to his county, and places a large order directly through the platform. Before logging off, he checks commodity price trends and lists a portion of his upcoming soybean harvest to secure a competitive pre-season contract. 

 

Finn: 

Finn’s client is going to host a dinner party with their friends and colleagues thus Finn is tasked to make a 3-course meal for all the guests along with appetizers and desserts. Thus, they write out and plan what will be served during the dinner party. Once they figure out the food items they need to purchase, they create a budget. To determine who to buy produce from, he logs into the farmer’s marketplace website. He searches for each item he needs and compares prices between sellers, specifically when it comes to bulk purchases. Through this, they’re able to find and purchase food items that fit within their budget.  

 

Jenny: 

Jenny harvest fresh produce in the morning, and lists it on the marketplace customers are able to view ratings and reviews of the items she posted. After some purchases, a buyer shares a link to one of Jennys products to her friends. These friends in turn buy more items from the seller Jenny. Jenny then takes a quick survey on products to better help the platform recommend items to her customers.  

 

Bucky: 

Bucky Birch, the operations manager of a large farm, notices that his farm has excess heirloom tomatoes after wholesale orders are fulfilled. Instead of letting the extra produce go to waste, he logs into the Local Farm Marketplace and creates a new listing. He uploads photos, sets the quantity available, and chooses pickup times for customers. As orders come in, the inventory updates automatically so customers cannot buy more than what is in stock. At the end of the day, Bucky checks the sales dashboard to review revenue and is satisfied that the platform helped move surplus product efficiently.
